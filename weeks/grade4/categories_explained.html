Weekly Math Challenge

Ponderosa Elementary. Grade 4

How the Challenge works

Weekly Math Challenge pilot program is an optional free math enrichment program for Ponderosa students (grade 4 and grade 1). It consists of a series of challenging and interesting grade-appropriate problems that encourage critical thinking. All the problems are similar in style to those found in math contests such as Math Kangaroo and Noetic Math. The Weekly Math Challenge will run for 12 weeks (with one extra week of bonus problems). Each week will have 5 or 6 problems across five cognitive skills (Logical Reasoning, Geometry and Spatial Sense, Combinatorics, Number and Operations and Word Problems).

Students can work online or on a printed copy, and answers are due each Friday afternoon. Each student submits once, and each problem is worth 1 point. Appearing on the leaderboard is a student's choice. Teachers can see every solution from the start; students see a week's solutions once that week has closed. Teachers are not expected to grade the students' work. The online submissions will be graded automatically while the papers will be collected every Friday and graded by an organizer. At the end of the 12 weeks the students that worked hard and consistently can be rewarded and acknowledged in some way (TBD).
